## Voqalize Says Voice Is Becoming the Default Way People Use Software
Bengaluru (Karnataka) [India], October 22: Voqalize today said it gives existing applications, websites and AI agents a voice interface that can listen, see context, respond and act, without rebuilding the application. The company says teams can have it running in about an hour, either through Plug & Play, where Voqalize manages the AI agent and the voice experience, or through a developer integration into their own backend.
