## In 2022, New York’s Great Northern Mall closed after 34 years of business, and four years later developer Guy Hart Jr has completed its demolition to make way for a community with nearly 1,700 homes,
The Great Northern Mall in Clay, N.Y. has been torn down to initiate a new development project that promises to reshape the community. Plans include nearly 1,700 homes, plus shopping and hotel options, alongside a medical campus to serve the public and generate jobs. This overhaul is anticipated to drive local economic growth, although additional planning and approvals are still pending before breaking ground on this exciting venture.
