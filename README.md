## ESBEE ropes in Sharad Kelkar as brand ambassador, launches 'Spaces That Breathe' campaign
Pune (Maharashtra) [India], November 17: Pune-based real estate developer ESBEE has appointed actor Sharad Kelkar as its brand ambassador and launched its new brand campaign, 'Spaces That Breathe'. Built around natural light, ventilation, greenery, and openness, the campaign explores how the design of a space shapes the way people live, unwind, and spend time with the people who matter to them.
