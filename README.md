## Starts Construction of Its First Residential Project, The Oryza
Gurugram (Haryana) [India], October 29: Origen Realty, a leading real estate developer in Delhi-NCR, has kick-started construction of The Oryza, its first residential project. The company has partnered with Indigo Infra projects Pvt. Ltd., a civil engineering and geotechnical construction company specialising in foundation, substructure and ground engineering works, for the execution of diaphragm wall construction.
